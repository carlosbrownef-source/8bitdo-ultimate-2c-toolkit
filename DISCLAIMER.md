# Before you start

**This is a vibe-coded project.** It was written with an AI assistant (Opus 5.5) and tested on one controller and one dongle, my own. It should work on the same models and firmware, unless there are new hardware versions.

**It comes with NO WARRANTY of any kind, explicit or implied**, including any warranty of merchantability or fitness
for a particular purpose. You use it entirely at your own risk. The program modifies the app portion of the controller's and the dongle's memory, and makes a lot of assumptions about how the firmware works. It has been tested as working, but not extensively, and edge cases may exist.

**Installing it probably voids your controller's and dongle's warranty.** You can restore your firmware to the original version, but I cannot know if it's possible for 8BitDo to tell you modified it anyway.

**It only works with the 8BitDo Ultimate 2C Wireless controller running firmware 1.09, and its 2.4 GHz dongle running
firmware 1.03.** It does not work with other 8BitDo models or with other firmware versions, and it won't let you continue if it doesn't detect the correct device and firmware.

**It will probably break compatibility with 8BitDo's own apps**, both the Firmware Updater and Ultimate Software (the controller and the dongle report themselves differently).

**You can always return to the original firmware.** The part of the controller that runs update mode (its bootloader)
is never modified, and Restore restores either your own backup or 8BitDo's original firmware: 1.09 for the controller, 1.03 for the dongle.
